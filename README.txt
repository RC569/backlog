My music backlog.

Status

- Not Downloaded: Album hasn't been downloaded yet
- Unplayed: Album is downloaded, not sorted in my library, and I haven't listened to it
- In Library: Album is sorted in my library, and I haven't listened to it
- Relisten: Album is sorted in my library, and I want to relisten to it

Priority

- Low, Medium, High, Next
- Next are albums I want to listen to next
- Next with asterisks are what I want to listen to in order
- For example: Next -> Next* -> Next**
